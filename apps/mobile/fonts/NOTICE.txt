Dinaya Android ships two OFL typefaces to match dinaya.lk:

- Cal Sans SemiBold — display, headings, wordmark, nav, buttons
  Source: src/fonts/CalSans-SemiBold.woff2 (converted to TTF)
  Copyright 2021 The Cal Sans Project Authors (https://github.com/calcom/font)

- Inter Regular / Medium / SemiBold / Bold — body copy and form fields
  Source: Inter 4.1 extras/ttf from https://github.com/rsms/inter
  See OFL-Inter.txt
