## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BRAND IDENTITY

### What is Dinaya?
**Dinaya** is Sri Lanka’s premier booking, deposit collection, and business growth platform built specifically for local service businesses (salons, barbershops, spas, bridal makeup artists, skin/aesthetic clinics, wellness studios, tuition, and specialized service professionals).

It solves the single biggest bottleneck holding back Sri Lankan service businesses: **the manual WhatsApp and Instagram DM booking chaos.**

## 2. THE SRI LANKAN BUSINESS REALITY & THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

### The "WhatsApp & DM Chaos"
In Sri Lanka, over 75% of businesses are SMEs. Almost all beauty salons, barbers, bridal artists, aesthetic doctors, and personal trainers market visually on Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook. 

However, their booking flow is completely broken:
1. **The Midnight DM Dilemma:** An Instagram follower wants an appointment. They DM at 11:30 PM: *"Hi sis, hair color price please? Available this Saturday?"* The owner is asleep or exhausted. By the time they reply the next morning, the client has booked elsewhere.
2. **The 20-Message Ping-Pong:** It takes 15 to 20 messages back and forth just to agree on a date, time, service, and staff member.
3. **No-Shows Eat 20–30% of Profits:** Clients casually book over chat, and then never show up. The salon held the chair for 2 hours, turned away other paying clients, and lost revenue. Chasing deposits manually via bank transfer screenshots is awkward and time-consuming.
4. **Double Bookings & Human Calendar Traps:** Owners or front-desk staff write appointments in physical paper diaries or messy Google Sheets. Two stylists get booked for the same chair at 2 PM, causing embarrassing customer conflicts.
5. **Awkward Payment Reconciliation:** *"Can you WhatsApp me the bank transfer receipt?"* Then the owner has to log into their banking app to verify if the Rs. 3,500 actually arrived.

### The Dinaya Solution: Order from Chaos
With Dinaya:
- The business puts `yourname.dinaya.lk` in their **Instagram Bio**, **WhatsApp Auto-Reply**, and on an **acrylic QR stand at the counter**.
- Clients tap the link, see the full service menu with exact prices, pick their preferred stylist/specialist, select an open time slot, and pay a deposit (or full amount) seamlessly via **PayHere** (Visa, Mastercard, LankaQR).
- The client receives instant SMS/WhatsApp confirmation.
- The owner's phone pings: *"New Booking: Kavya booked Haircut & Style for Thu 10:30 AM · Deposit Rs. 1,250 received."*
- 24 hours and 2 hours before the appointment, Dinaya automatically sends smart reminders.
- **Result:** No-shows drop to near zero, staff know exactly who is arriving, and owners get their evenings back.

## 3. CORE PRODUCT PILLARS & FEATURES

1. **Public Booking Hub (`yourname.dinaya.lk`)**
   - Clean, lightning-fast, mobile-optimized booking flow (takes under 30 seconds for a client to book).
   - Shows real-time available time slots based on staff schedules and working hours.
   - Embeddable into existing websites or shareable as a standalone link.
   - Can map to custom domains on Growth plans (e.g., `book.salonluxe.lk`).

2. **PayHere Payments & Deposits (0% Platform Commission)**
   - Fully integrated with Sri Lanka’s top payment gateway (**PayHere**).
   - Businesses can require a 20%, 50%, or 100% deposit to lock in a booking.
   - Supports credit/debit cards and LankaQR.
   - Dinaya **never takes a percentage commission** on appointments (unlike international platforms that take 10–25%).

3. **Automated Reminders (WhatsApp & SMS)**
   - Sends automatic confirmations and reminder alerts before appointments.
   - Includes one-tap reschedule or cancellation token links so clients can manage their own slots without calling.

4. **Owner & Staff Dashboard**
   - Real-time interactive calendar (day, week, staff view).
   - Google Calendar two-way synchronization.
   - Client management database (CRM) with booking history, spend, notes, and preferences.
   - Multi-staff and multi-branch management.

5. **Dinaya Deals (The Slow-Day Filler)**
   - Allows owners to publish limited flash discounts for quiet hours (e.g., *"Tuesday 10 AM – 1 PM: 20% off Facials"*).
   - Fills empty chairs without having to blast cheap public discounts that devalue the brand.

6. **Concierge Setup ("Send Us Your Menu")**
   - The biggest barrier for Sri Lankan salon owners is: *"Aiyoh, I don't have time to set up software."*
   - Dinaya offers **Done-For-You Concierge Onboarding**: The owner simply takes a photo of their paper menu or WhatsApp price list and sends it to the Dinaya team. Dinaya sets up the entire booking page within 24 hours.

7. **AI Growth Machine (Growth Tier)**
   - **AI Review Engine & Replies:** Automatically requests reviews after visits and drafts professional, personalized responses.
   - **Client Reactivation Campaigns:** Identifies clients who haven’t visited in 60 days and drafts tailored WhatsApp reactivation offers.
   - **AI Content Machine:** Helps owners draft social media post ideas and promotions.
   - *(Note on AI: Positioned as an operational accelerator, never as speculative hype).*
